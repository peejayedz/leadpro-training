1Welcome

LeadPro.Legal brings every new lead into one place: website forms, phone calls, texts and chat. Your team can answer quickly, book consultations and see which leads turn into signed cases.

  1. Answer new leads fast. The sooner a lead hears from you, the more likely they are to book.
  2. Move each lead to the right stage as things happen. This keeps your scorecard accurate.
  3. Check your calendar for upcoming consultations.
Tip: Everything in this guide is in the menu on the left side of your account.

2Logging in

Each person on your team has their own login.

  1. Go to app.lawfirmmarketingpros.com and bookmark it.
  2. Sign in with your email and password. First time? Use the link in your invite email to set your password.
  3. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password on the login page and follow the email.
Tip: Don't share logins. If someone new joins your team, ask us to add them so they get their own.

3Your dashboard

Menu → Dashboard

A quick view of how your leads are doing.

  1. See how many new leads came in, how many were contacted and how many booked a consultation.
  2. Use the date picker at the top to change the period, for example this week or last month.
  3. For more detail, open Reporting in the menu.
Tip: The numbers are only as good as your pipeline. Keep lead stages up to date (section 7).

4Your KPI scorecard

Your scorecard shows every step from new lead to signed client, compared with goals set for your firm, so you can see where leads drop off and what to fix first. Your CSM will show you where to open it in your account.

How to read it

  1. The summary at the top shows what's going well, what needs attention, and our recommended focus for the coming weeks.
  2. Lead → signed client is the big number: the share of new leads that became clients. Cost per signed case divides your ad spend by the cases signed.
  3. Where leads drop off follows your leads step by step, from New leads → Contacted → Qualified → Consult booked → Consult held → Agreement sent → Signed. The biggest drop is usually the best place to improve.
  4. Use the date buttons to change the period, and Compare to prior to see the period before.

The four areas

1 · Leads inNew leads, speed to first contact, how many were contacted within 5 minutes, and missed calls not returned.
2 · IntakeContact rate, how many leads qualified, and why leads were turned away.
3 · ConsultationsHow many qualified leads booked, the show rate and the no-show rate.
4 · Signed clientsFee agreements sent, how many were signed, and total signed clients.

What the badges mean

On goal The number meets your firm's goal. Off goal Below goal. Look here first. Too few Fewer than 5 leads, so the rate isn't meaningful yet.

Each rate also shows the real count beside it, for example "81% · 39 of 48". Goals are set for your firm and practice area, and we review them with you every quarter. Leads by source at the bottom shows which channels bring leads and signed clients, such as Google Ads, Local Services Ads or referrals.

Your pipeline feeds the scorecard

The scorecard counts leads by their stage in your pipeline (section 7). If a lead isn't moved, the scorecard can't count it.

5Conversations

Menu → Conversations

Your inbox for texts, emails, website chats and call history, all in one place.

  1. New and unread messages are at the top. Click one to open the conversation.
  2. Type your reply at the bottom and choose how to send it: SMS or Email.
  3. Click the phone icon to call the lead from your account.
  4. Use the contact details on the right to see who the person is and their history with your firm.
Tip: Reply from here rather than your personal phone, so the whole team can see the conversation.

6Contacts

Menu → Contacts

Every lead and client is a contact.

  1. Search by name, phone number or email at the top of the list.
  2. Open a contact to see their details, notes, messages, appointments and where they came from.
  3. Add a note after every call, so anyone on your team can pick up where you left off.
Tip: Before you call a lead back, check the notes. Someone may already have spoken with them.

7Your lead pipeline

Menu → Opportunities

The pipeline shows where every lead is, from first contact to signed case. Move a lead by dragging its card to the next column, or open the card and change its stage.

2Phone leadIt came in by phone. New phone leads usually land here automatically.
3Lead Contacted?Someone on your team has reached the lead.
4Lead Qualified?The lead fits the cases you take.

6Showed: Agreement SentThey attended and you've sent the agreement.
7Showed: Lead RejectedThey attended but the case isn't a fit.

Tip: Your stage names may differ slightly. Moving leads the same day something happens keeps your KPI scorecard and follow-ups accurate.

8Calendar

Menu → Calendars

Your booked consultations, in one calendar.

  1. Open Calendars to see consultations by day, week or month.
  2. Click an appointment to see who it's with, reschedule it or change its status, for example to Showed or No-show.
  3. Connect your Google or Outlook calendar so busy times are blocked and new consultations show on your own calendar: go to Calendars → Calendar settings → Connections, then click + Add new.
Tip: Each person connects their own calendar while signed in with their own login.

9Mobile app & alerts

Answer leads from your phone, even when you're away from your desk.

  1. Install the LeadConnector app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Sign in with the same email and password you use on the computer.
  3. When asked, allow notifications, so you're alerted the moment a new lead or message arrives.
Tip: Ask your CSM which notifications to turn on. Most firms want alerts for new leads and new messages.
